THE VIRUS (V-7)

* Origin: Voss's research on enhanced strength produced a strain that only takes hold in people with low vitality (the sick, the elderly, the exhausted).
* Early infected are slow and weak. As the virus mutates, later infected are stronger.
* Bite spread: an infected bite infects the victim. After a delay the victim turns.
* Strength boost: infection makes a person stronger than they were in life. A former dockworker or guard turns into a far more dangerous enemy than an elderly resident.
* Reborn rule: if a normal infected is killed without a head destruction, fire, or a melee finisher, it shrieks and rises again as a Reborn with tentacle limbs that attack at low to mid range.
* Leon infection meter: when bitten, an infection meter rises slowly. It lowers Leon's maximum health and adds shaking, heavy breathing and screen distortion until he uses a serum or treatment item.
* The virus grows stronger through the chapters. The same enemy type is tougher in later chapters.

ENEMIES

* Husk: slow walker, basic infected.
* Reborn: tentacle form after an unfinished kill. Low to mid range lashes.
* Drowned: water-based infected that grabs from shallows and flooded streets.
* Brute: armored, slow, hits hard. Appears from Chapter 3.
