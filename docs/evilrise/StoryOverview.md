# EvilRise: story overview and cast

## The story in one page

Port Halvern is the container port for the city of Marrow Bay. Vigor Biotech holds the city's water treatment contract and runs a lab tower downtown. Eight months ago, a V-7 trial at the company's sealed research annex under Saltmere Island went wrong. Vigor moved the work into the tower and shipped what was left of the annex stock through the port, labelled as medical supplies.

At 02:14 on the night before the story, two night workers pried open container VGR-7718 in Yard C. Cold mist spilled out. Bert Linnane, the old, sick night watchman, sat down in it and turned first. He bit Dan Kelso, the big dockworker who came back to help him, and Kelso turned faster and stronger. That is the rule of V-7. Breathing it in only takes hold in the weak. A bite gives a full dose to anyone, and strong bodies come back stronger. By dawn the port was lost. Harbor radio operator Mara Quinn called for help from Saltmere Point. A three-agent federal contact team led by Dale Pruitt landed at 13:00. At 17:40 Pruitt reported that "port police" were taking the container, and then he went silent.

**Chapter 1, Port Halvern (22:30).** Agent Leon Cater lands alone on the north pier. Mara finds him on the port radio and talks him through the port. In the dock office he plays the overnight security tape and watches the outbreak begin. He crosses Warehouse 3 and learns the Reborn rule: a body that is not finished gets back up. He restores crane power and kills the Hookman, the night crane operator Dmitri Varga. Then he takes the port tender across the river.

**Chapter 2, Saltmere Island (00:40).** In a flooded fishing town, Leon follows Dr. Ines Calder's hidden diary. Calder was Voss's assistant and is now drowned. A church organ, a bell tower and the lighthouse lens lead him to a stair under the lighthouse. A photo ties Police Chief Warren Hale to Dr. Elias Voss. The Brine Maw hunts the harbor.

**Chapter 3, the Annex (02:10).** This is the sealed lab where it started. Patient Zero's footage shows the first infection. Mara comes down through the church crypt lift and finds her brother Owen dead with the B2 keycard. Her secret comes out: Owen warned her about the container and she said nothing. Leon learns the cold-shock sequence and takes antidote component A from the cold vault. Hale's signature is on the shipment clearance. The Warden falls. Mara rows the raft out through the sea cave past the Undertow.

**Chapter 4, Marrow Bay (04:00).** The city is half gone. A dead officer's body camera shows Hale's men taking Pruitt at the port. Mara holds the police station lobby on the relay radio. Leon finds the payments ledger and finds Pruitt, bitten and dying in the cells. Pruitt gives him floor 35. Hale was bitten at the port and has run out of stolen suppressant. He turns, and he dies. The sewer key leads under the city.

**Chapter 5, the Vigor Tower (05:20).** Voss plans to put concentrated V-7 into the city's water at sunrise, 06:12. Leon takes components B and C from floors 31 and 33. On floor 35 he runs the synthesis with the cold-shock sequence. Voss injects himself and becomes the final fight. The antidote goes out through Voss's own dosing line instead. Mara gives the all-clear at first light. One vial in the sample ledger is unaccounted for.

## Cast sheet

| Who | Role | Want | Arc |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Leon Cater** (player) | Federal agent, ex-tactical, mid-30s. Calm, dry, controlled. | Find the contact team and stop what started at the port. | Arrives alone, finishes things others left halfway, and keeps his word to Pruitt. |
| **Mara Quinn** | Harbor radio operator on Saltmere Point. | Keep Leon alive, and keep her secret. | Voice on the radio (Ch1 and Ch2), at his side in the Annex and on the raft (Ch3), on the station relay (Ch4 and Ch5). Owns her guilt about Owen in Ch3. |
| **Dr. Elias Voss** | Vigor Biotech's lead scientist. | To "lift" the weak overnight through the city water. | Unseen until Ch5, then a man, then the final boss in three phases. |
| **Chief Warren Hale** | Marrow Bay police chief, paid by Vigor. | Bury the outbreak and his part in it. | His guilt builds over three chapters: a photo (Ch2), a signature (Ch3), the ledger and a confession (Ch4). Boss in Ch4. |
| **Dr. Ines Calder** | Voss's assistant, drowned three weeks ago. | Leave the truth where someone would find it. | Speaks only through her diary and Voss's message. |
| **Agent Dale Pruitt** | Leader of the lost contact team. | Call it in. | Clues in Ch1 (crushed radio, the cage code). Found in the Ch4 cells. Leon keeps his promise off-screen. |
| **Owen Quinn** | Mara's brother, a Vigor lab technician. | Warn his sister. | Found dead in the Annex in Ch3 with the keycard. |
| **Dmitri Varga, the Hookman** | Night operator of Crane 3. | Stay in the shed so he only hurts the ones that come in. | Ch1 boss. |
| **Bert Linnane** | Port watchman, 71. | Finish his round. | The first to turn, on the tape. |
| **Dan Kelso** | Dockworker. | Help the old man. | Bitten on the tape. Comes back as a strong Husk in Ch1. |
| **Tom Begg** | Night worker who opened the container. | Get off shift. | Seen only on the tape and in his unsent texts. |
| **Hank Doyle** | Boat pilot. | Get paid and get out. | Drops Leon at the pier in Ch1. |
| **Lena Ortiz, Sam Whitlock** | Pruitt's team. | Hold the line. | Found dead: Ortiz in Ch1, Whitlock in Ch4. |
| **Ezra Penhallow** | Subject 7, Patient Zero. | Nothing left. | Ch3 footage. |
| **Officer Ana Ruiz** | Marrow Bay officer. | Record what she saw. | Her body camera is the Ch4 evidence. |
