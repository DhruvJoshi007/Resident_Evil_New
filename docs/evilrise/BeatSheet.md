## Prologue: Check-In

The night before Chapter 1. No infected are on screen until the morning: the first ten minutes are the story of the virus, a hotel lobby and a quiet room. Then the window, the knock, and a stealth escape that teaches the Prologue's one rule: Husks only come for what they see, and if you break their line of sight they forget you. It ends with a drive, not a fight.

## Chapter 1: Port Halvern

22:30. Rain, sodium floods, and a container port that has stopped answering its own radio. Leon Cater comes in by hired boat because the harbour authority stopped returning calls eighteen hours ago, and because a three-person federal contact team walked in here at noon yesterday and never walked out. Chapter 1 teaches the whole grammar of the game in order: look, read, sneak, finish what you kill, solve, then fight because there is no other door. The investigation is the spine — manifests in the dock office tie sealed Vigor Biotech freight to Yard C, and the dock office CCTV holds the only recording of the night everything started. The reveal is small and ugly and arrives on a monitor, not in a speech: something was shipped in, it was opened at 02:14, and somebody at this port had already been told not to open it.

The chapter's emotional job is to put Mara Quinn in Leon's ear and let her slip once. After the tape she says her brother's name before she can stop herself. Leon lets it go. The player does not.

## Chapter 2: Saltmere Island

00:40. Leon ties off the port tender from Halvern at the Saltmere landing and walks into a town that the sea has taken back. The streets are knee to waist deep, the fog is low, and the only moving lights are the lighthouse sweep and one small window out on Saltmere Point: Mara, in the harbor radio station, close enough to see and impossible to reach. The water is the enemy here. The Drowned wait in it, so Leon moves house to house, learns to hide, and follows the trail of a dead woman's diary. Dr. Ines Calder hid her pages in the church, the fish market and her own cellar three weeks before the harbor gave her back drowned. Together they say the thing the port never did: V-7 was not built as a weapon. It was built as a treatment for weak bodies, and Voss called the result a success. A water-stained photograph in her front room says something smaller and worse, that Voss and Chief Hale have been shaking hands for years. The chapter turns on two machines and one monster: a chapel organ that hides the bell tower key, a lighthouse lens that hides a service latch, and the Brine Maw in the harbor basin, which can only be beaten by closing the sluice gate and taking the water away from it. The last page points under Leon's feet, to a research annex below the island, and the lighthouse stair takes him down to it.

## Chapter 3: The Annex

02:10. The hidden stair under the Saltmere lighthouse drops into a service tunnel, and the tunnel ends at a bulkhead stamped with a Vigor Biotech serial. Behind it is the research annex that was sealed eight months ago and left off every map. Chapter 3 is the chapter where the game stops being a rescue and becomes a case: the footage reels in the patient ward show how V-7 began, the trial log gives Leon the first thing that can be used against it (a cold-shock ladder of 4 °C, −12 °C, 4 °C), and a shipment clearance in the security office carries Chief Hale's countersignature. It is also the chapter where Mara stops being a voice. She rides the church crypt lift down to find Leon, and twenty metres further on they find her brother Owen dead in a flooded stairwell with a Level B2 keycard in his hand. She has been carrying that since the dock office. It comes out quietly, between two people who still have to keep moving.

Pacing runs explore and learn (tunnel, reception, ward), solve (keycard, whiteboard code), hide (flooded lower levels, Drowned and the first Brute), fight when cornered (coolant plant), boss (The Warden), escape (sea cave), sea boss (The Undertow), escape 2 (the Marrow Bay shore).

## Chapter 4: Marrow Bay

04:00. The raft grounds on a shingle shore under the harbour wall, and Leon is not alone now: Mara comes ashore with him. The city above them is half gone and half still moving. Buses sit empty with their doors open, sandbags block a plaza that nobody is defending any more, and the things in the flooded underpasses are stronger than anything in the port was. Leon's target is the Marrow Bay police station, because the station is where the paperwork lives, and the paperwork is the only thing left that can tell him who let the container through.

The chapter is an investigation with teeth. Officer Ana Ruiz's body camera, found in the street, shows Hale's own officers taking Agent Pruitt at the port — which means Pruitt was never missing, he was taken. Inside the station, Mara stays in the lobby safe room on the relay radio while Leon works the building: archive, evidence room, detective office, holding cells, chief's office. Three locked steps stand between him and Hale's safe, and behind the safe is the sewer route out. Between those steps, in cell 4, is Pruitt — bitten at the port, held on Hale's orders with no record, and almost out of time. Pruitt is the one who tells Leon where Voss is and what Voss means to do to the city's water at dawn. Leon keeps the promise he makes in that cell off-screen, and the chapter never mentions it again.

Hale is still in the building. He has been hiding a bite since the port, and the suppressant he stole from Vigor shipments ran out tonight. He confesses in his own dark office because there is nobody left to hide it from, then he comes apart in the flooded basement, and Leon has to use the building's own wiring to put him down. The chapter ends at a maintenance door under the station, with a works map that runs straight to Vigor Tower.

Enemy scaling in this chapter is ×1.5 (Canon F17). Ammo is deliberately thin on the street legs so the player arrives at the station short.

## Chapter 5: The Vigor Tower

05:20. Leon comes up out of the Marrow Bay storm sewers into the service level of Vigor Tower with Antidote Component A in his vest, taken from the Annex cold vault two hours ago. Mara stays behind at the police station relay with the city radio and the people sheltering in the lobby — she has the only working line to the waterworks crew, so she talks Leon through the tower instead of walking it with him. The tower is not overrun the way the city is. It is worse: it is tidy. Voss sent the night staff home, kept the ones who volunteered, and has been dosing them on camera. Below Leon's feet the building's own water plant holds four hundred litres of concentrated V-7 on a timer, aimed at the Marrow Bay supply (fix F15). Sunrise is 06:12. That is the clock.

Leon's work is a chain: component B from the Genetics vault on floor 31, component C from the cryo rack on floor 33, then both of them plus A into the synthesis machine in Voss's Crown Lab on floor 35, which will not run without the cold-shock sequence found in the Annex trial log (see Chapter 3, flag `FLG_C3_ColdShockKnown`, fix F12). Voss meets him there, in person, and then stops being a person. Infected in this chapter scale ×1.7 (fix F17), ammo is thin after the station, and the Reborn rule still applies to Husks and Drowned (fix F4) — the lab staff were strong, well-fed and dosed on purpose, so almost nothing here dies politely. The chapter ends on the roof at first light, with Voss's own dosing line pushing the antidote into the city instead, Mara's all-clear on the radio, and one number in a sample ledger that does not add up.
