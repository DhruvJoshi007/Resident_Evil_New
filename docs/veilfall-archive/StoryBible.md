# VEILFALL: Story Bible

## Logline

A search-and-rescue officer enters a quarantined coastal city to find her missing brother. She learns that the outbreak was a weapons test, and that her brother's blood may be the only cure.

## Setting: Harrow Bay

Harrow Bay is a mid-sized coastal city built on a river delta. It is grey, rainy and post-industrial, and its economy depends on one employer: **Veyl Biomedical**.

| Location | Description | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| Harrow Bay Police Precinct | A converted 1920s courthouse with a cell block, archives, and a statue hall from its courthouse days. | 1 |
| Kettle Street | Shopping streets around the precinct, barricaded and burning. | 1 |
| Storm drains and sewers | Victorian brick tunnels under the old town. | 2 |
| Delta Water Treatment Plant | A 1970s plant on the river, where Veyl rented storage space. | 2 |
| Gallow Island | A fishing village and lighthouse 3 km offshore, depopulated 8 years ago after a "fish disease". | 3 |
| Veyl Station Zero | A hidden lab under the lighthouse, Veyl's original test site. | 3 |
| St. Marrow General Hospital | The city hospital, whose research wing is funded by Veyl. | 4 |
| Veyl Tower | A 40-floor glass headquarters with a reactor-level bio-lab underground. | 5 |

## The outbreak: Strain V

- **What it is:** a parasite-virus hybrid, codenamed **V-STRAIN** in Veyl files. It began as a regeneration therapy. In living hosts it rebuilds tissue, but it destroys the frontal brain, which leaves aggression and hunger.
- **Stages of infection:**
  1. **Carrier:** fever, nosebleeds, grey veins (1 to 12 hours)
  2. **Turned:** the shambler zombie
  3. **Adapted:** a host that has fed enough mutates (runners, listeners, crawlers)
  4. **Bloom:** large-scale mutation in hosts with high doses (the bosses)
- **How it spread:** Veyl stored waste canisters in the water treatment plant. A leak, which Veyl covered up, contaminated the water supply. When the outbreak started, Adrian Veyl ordered the city sealed and turned it into a live field test.
- **The cure:** a rare antibody. Theo Kessler has it naturally. Mara has a weaker version, which is why the stalker hunts her.

## Main characters

### Mara Kessler (protagonist)
- 31, Harrow Bay Search and Rescue officer and a former paramedic.
- Practical, stubborn and dry-humoured. Not a soldier, so she fights because she has to.
- **Goal:** find her younger brother Theo, who stopped answering calls three days ago.
- **Arc:** she starts out only wanting to save one person and ends up responsible for the whole city.
- **Gameplay link:** her paramedic background explains why she can combine herbs and treat injuries.

### Theo Kessler (missing brother)
- 24, a lab technician at Veyl. Kind, nervous, and the first to find out about the leak.
- Leaves voice messages and notes for Mara across the chapters, a trail of breadcrumbs.
- He is immune. Adrian captured him to use his blood to make an airborne version of the strain that hosts survive, which would make it sellable as a weapon.

### Sergeant Owen Pike (radio guide)
- 52, a police dispatcher holed up in the precinct radio tower.
- Guides Mara by radio. He is warm and funny, and slowly getting sick.
- He dies (off-screen, over the radio) at the end of Chapter 3, after giving Mara the last thing she needs.

### Dr. Ilse Brandt (guilty scientist)
- 58, V-Strain's original creator. She wanted to cure degenerative disease.
- First heard in audio logs (Chapter 2), met in person in Chapter 4.
- Gives Mara the vaccine formula. She is killed by Crane in Chapter 4.

### Julian Crane (traitor)
- 40, a Veyl security contractor. Friendly and capable, he presents himself as a fellow survivor.
- Helps Mara in Chapters 2 and 3. In Chapter 4 he steals the vaccine sample, kills Brandt and reveals he works for Adrian.
- Mara faces him again in Chapter 5, where he is infected by the Revenant during the chaos.

### Adrian Veyl (antagonist and final boss)
- 45, Veyl's Chief Science Officer and son of the founder.
- Calm and convincing. He believes the strain is the next step for humanity, and that selling it to militaries will fund that future.
- **Plan:** use Theo's antibody to make an airborne strain that hosts survive, release it over the city as a demonstration for buyers, and then sell it.
- In the final fight he injects himself with the pure "Bloom" strain.

### The Revenant (stalker)
- Veyl's prototype bio-weapon: a 2.4 m tall host in a reinforced containment suit.
- Programmed to find and bring back anyone with the antibody, which means Theo, and now Mara.
- Cannot be killed until Chapter 5. It can be stunned (heavy damage, flash grenades). It follows sound and opens doors, but it cannot enter safe rooms.

## Mystery structure

| Chapter | Question it answers | Question it raises |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Quarantine | **What** is happening? People are turning, and the city is sealed on purpose. | Where did it start? |
| 2 Undercurrent | **How** did it start? Veyl waste got into the water supply. | Why is Veyl hiding it, and why does Theo's trail lead to an island? |
| 3 Hollow Isle | **Who** did it? Adrian Veyl, who ran human tests on Gallow Island years ago. | What does Adrian want with Theo? |
| 4 St. Marrow | **Why**? An airborne weapon that needs Theo's antibody. Crane is revealed as a traitor. | Can Mara stop the release? |
| 5 Veyl Tower | **How do we stop it?** Make the vaccine, stop the release and defeat Adrian. | Sequel tease: the strain samples were already sold. |

## Themes

- **Family:** Mara and Theo, against Adrian and the father whose legacy he wants to outdo.
- **Corporate cover-up:** the people paid to protect the city sold it out.
- **Guilt and repair:** Brandt made the strain, and she tries to undo it.

## Tone

- Grounded horror, not campy. There is humour, mostly from Pike and Mara, but it is used sparingly.
- Gore exists to support dread, not shock.
- Quiet moments matter: notes from victims, abandoned homes, and Theo's voice messages.

## Ending

Mara synthesises the vaccine using Theo's blood, defeats Adrian's Bloom form, and stops the release. The vaccine is fired into the city's emergency sprinkler and water systems from the tower. Theo survives, weakened. In the post-credits scene, an unknown buyer opens a case holding three V-Strain vials, which sets up the sequel.

## Naming rules

- No names, places, logos or companies from Resident Evil or any other existing franchise.
- Enemy names are informal, as Mara or Pike would describe them ("listeners", "runners").
- Veyl documents use clinical codes such as "V-STRAIN", "SUBJECT GI-07" and "BLOOM PROTOCOL".
