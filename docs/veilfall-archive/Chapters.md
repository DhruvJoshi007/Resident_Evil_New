# VEILFALL: Chapter Breakdown (first build, 5 chapters, about 2 hours)

Every chapter follows the same shape (see [ChapterTemplate.md](ChapterTemplate.md)):

**Arrival cutscene → explore and investigate → hints → puzzles → shortcuts → special room → stealth / chase / combat → boss → escape cutscene**

| # | Chapter | Location | Time | New weapon | New enemy | Boss | Escape route |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quarantine | Police precinct and Kettle Street | 25 min | Shotgun | Shambler | The Warden | Sewer gate under the cells |
| 2 | Undercurrent | Sewers and water treatment plant | 25 min | Hunting rifle | Listener, Larvae | The Mother | Motorboat down the river to the island |
| 3 | Hollow Isle | Gallow Island village and lighthouse | 25 min | Grenade launcher | Village Elder, Crawler | The Fisherman | Lighthouse elevator down into the lab |
| 4 | St. Marrow | Hospital and research wing | 20 min | SMG | Runner | Twin Surgeons | Door to rooftop, then cable car |
| 5 | Veyl Tower | Corporate HQ and bio-lab | 25 min | Magnum | Veyl Soldier | Revenant (final form), then Adrian Veyl | Timed escape to the helipad |

Mara starts with a **knife**, a **handgun** (9 rounds), **1 green herb** and a **flashlight**.

## Chapter 1: Quarantine

**Question answered:** What is happening?
**Setting:** night, heavy rain. Harrow Bay Police Precinct, a former courthouse, and Kettle Street outside it.

### Flow
1. **Arrival cutscene (C1-01):** Mara's rescue truck is turned back at the quarantine wall. She climbs in through a drainage culvert and walks onto Kettle Street, where the barricades are burning.
2. **Kettle Street (tutorial):** movement, flashlight, the first file and the first breakable crate. Mara sees a shambler feeding in an alley, and the player learns to crouch past it (stealth tutorial).
3. **Precinct lobby (safe room 1):** save point, item box. Pike calls on the radio from the tower. The main door locks behind her.
4. **Investigate:** the West Office, Archives, Statue Hall, and Interrogation rooms. The player finds files about the "riots" and Theo's missing person report, which Mara filed herself.
5. **First forced fight (C1-02 cutscene):** an officer turns in front of Mara in the briefing room. The player learns to shoot and aim for the legs.
6. **Puzzles:** collect three medallions (see below). Restoring power opens the East wing shortcut back to the lobby.
7. **Special room:** the Chief's Armoury. Get the **Shotgun**, the **Cell Block Key** and the **District Map**.
8. **Cell block:** shamblers in the cells. A save point sits outside the boss room.
9. **Boss: The Warden.**
10. **Escape cutscene (C1-06):** the Warden falls through the cell floor, and the floor collapses to reveal a sewer gate. Pike: "Theo's car was found by the river. Follow the water."

### Puzzles
| Puzzle | Hint source | Solution | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lion Medallion | File "Night Shift Log": the lion "faces the one who judges". | Rotate the Statue Hall lion to face the judge's bench. | Lion Medallion |
| Owl Medallion | Locker combination in a dead officer's notebook (the code is the badge number on his body, 0-4-1-7). | Open locker 12 in the West Office. | Owl Medallion |
| Serpent Medallion | Fuse box: route power through 3 breakers. The wiring diagram is in the Archives. | Breakers B, D and E on; A and C off. | Power to Interrogation, then the Serpent Medallion in the evidence locker |
| Armoury door | The three medallion slots on the Chief's door. | Place all 3 medallions. | Special room opens |

### Enemies
Shamblers (10 to 14 in total). One "dead" body in the West Office gets up on the way back.

### Boss: The Warden
- A former jail guard, Bloom-mutated, with a riot shield fused to his arm and a baton.
- **Arena:** the cell block's central hall, two floors, with breakable cell doors.
- **Phase 1:** blocks with the shield. Shoot the exposed back pustules, or lure him into charging into cell bars, which stuns him.
- **Phase 2 (below 50% health):** the shield breaks off. He climbs to the upper floor and drops on Mara, and he releases shamblers from the cells.
- **Weak point:** the pulsing growth on his back and neck.

### Files (6) and audio logs (2)
1. Night Shift Log (Lion hint)
2. Missing Person Report: Theo Kessler
3. Memo: "Quarantine order. Do NOT engage Veyl personnel."
4. Officer Hale's notebook (locker hint)
5. Archives wiring diagram (fuse hint)
6. Chief Dunn's diary: "Veyl paid for the new wing. Now they tell us what to do."
- Audio log: Theo's voicemail 1, "Mara, don't come to the city. I found something at work."
- Audio log: precinct emergency broadcast loop.

## Chapter 2: Undercurrent

**Question answered:** How did it start?
**Setting:** sewers under the old town, then the Delta Water Treatment Plant on the river.

### Flow
1. **Arrival cutscene (C2-01):** Mara drops into the storm drains. The water is glowing faintly. Julian Crane appears, gun raised, then lowers it: "You're not one of them."
2. **Sewers:** a flooded maze in the dark. **Listeners** are introduced. They are blind, and the player must walk, not run, through the water (stealth section).
3. **Plant entrance (safe room 2):** Crane splits up from Mara to "find another way". The Trader is introduced here (sells and upgrades).
4. **Investigate:** Veyl shipping manifests, canister storage, and Dr. Brandt's first audio log.
5. **First Revenant appearance (C2-03):** it smashes through a wall in the pumping hall. **Chase:** run to the safe room or lose it with a flash grenade.
6. **Puzzles:** water valves, pipe rotation, weed-killer mixing.
7. **Special room:** the plant control room. Get the **Hunting Rifle**, the **Boat Key** and Theo's ID badge.
8. **Boss: The Mother.**
9. **Escape cutscene (C2-06):** Mara starts a motorboat at the docks. Boat-chase set piece downriver (on rails, with shooting). Crane radios that Theo was taken to Gallow Island.

### Puzzles
| Puzzle | Hint source | Solution | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water valves | Flow chart on the plant wall | Close valves 2 and 3, open 1, to drain the east tank room | Access to the Valve Handle |
| Pipe rotation | Path diagram on the control room door | Rotate 6 pipe tiles to connect the inlet to the outlet | Pressure restored, door to the canister store opens |
| Weed-killer | Lab note: "2 parts B, 1 part A, never C" | Mix chemicals in the lab station | Clears the infested vine passage |
| Control room | Keycard (from the canister store) plus the Valve Handle | Use both | Special room |

### Enemies
Shamblers, 6 Listeners, larvae swarms, and the Revenant (stalks the pumping hall area for the second half of the chapter).

### Boss: The Mother
- A swollen host merged with the settling tank, spitting larvae.
- **Arena:** a circular settling tank with a walkway that floods in stages.
- **Phase 1:** shoot the 4 glowing sacs while larvae swarm. Larvae can be stomped.
- **Phase 2:** the tank floods. Mara must reach the raised walkway and drop electrical cables into the water (an environmental kill), or shoot the exposed heart.

### Files (6) and audio logs (2)
1. Veyl shipping manifest: "Canisters VB-9, store under plant B-level."
2. Plant manager's email: "Leak reported. Veyl says it's harmless."
3. Flow chart (valve hint)
4. Lab note (weed-killer hint)
5. A worker's diary: "Water tastes like metal."
6. Theo's note to Mara, hidden in the control room: "Gallow Island. Station Zero."
- Audio log: Dr. Brandt, "Log 14. If the canisters breach, the city has days."
- Audio log: Theo's voicemail 2.

## Chapter 3: Hollow Isle

**Question answered:** Who did it?
**Setting:** dawn fog. Gallow Island's fishing village, church and lighthouse.

### Flow
1. **Arrival cutscene (C3-01):** the boat crashes on the rocks. The village looks abandoned, but there are fresh footprints.
2. **Village:** crawlers in the houses, and the **Village Elder**, a patrolling, unkillable, slow but deadly host. **Stealth:** hide in cupboards and under beds, and use bells as distractions.
3. **Chapel (safe room 3).**
4. **Camcorder tape (playable flashback):** play as a villager 8 years ago during Adrian's "vaccination" program.
5. **Puzzles:** church bells, tide chart, lighthouse lens.
6. **Special room:** the crypt under the church. Get the **Grenade Launcher** and the **Station Zero Key**.
7. **Boss: The Fisherman.**
8. **Escape cutscene (C3-06):** a hidden elevator inside the lighthouse goes down to Station Zero. In the lab, Mara finds Adrian Veyl's test records and a recent video of Theo strapped to a chair. Pike dies over the radio after giving her the hospital code. Crane radios: "They moved Theo to St. Marrow."

### Puzzles
| Puzzle | Hint source | Solution | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Church bells | Hymn sheet "Call of the Tide" with note marks | Ring bells 3-1-4-2 | Crypt stairway opens |
| Tide chart | Harbour master's tide table plus a clock | Set the harbour clock to low tide (4:40) | Sea cave access, Crypt Key |
| Lighthouse lens | Painting in the elder's house | Align 3 lens rings so the beam hits the cliff marker | Reveals the elevator shaft |

### Enemies
Crawlers, shamblers (villagers), the Village Elder (stalker for this chapter), and seagull swarms (a hazard).

### Boss: The Fisherman
- An amphibious mutant with a harpoon arm. It dives between attacks.
- **Arena:** a collapsing pier in a storm. Planks break when hit by explosives.
- **Phase 1:** it surfaces to throw its harpoon. Shoot it when it surfaces.
- **Phase 2:** it pulls Mara toward the water with the harpoon line. Cut the line with the knife (a quick-time event), then use the grenade launcher on its gills.
- **Kill:** drop the crane hook on it.

### Files (6) and audio logs (2)
1. "Vaccination Day" flyer, signed Dr. A. Veyl
2. Subject GI-07 report
3. Hymn sheet (bell hint)
4. Tide table (tide hint)
5. Elder's letter to his daughter
6. Station Zero personnel list (Adrian Veyl, Ilse Brandt, Julian Crane: "Security")
- Audio log: Adrian, "Gallow results are promising. Mortality is acceptable."
- Audio log: Pike's last call.

## Chapter 4: St. Marrow

**Question answered:** Why? This is where the betrayal is revealed.
**Setting:** St. Marrow General Hospital at night, with its power failing.

### Flow
1. **Arrival cutscene (C4-01):** Mara arrives through the underground parking garage. Brandt reaches her on the hospital intercom.
2. **Wards:** **Runners** are introduced. They are fast, so the player has to use doors and shoves.
3. **Pharmacy (safe room 4).**
4. **The Revenant hunts Mara through the wards (chase/stealth).** She breaks line of sight using the laundry chutes and the pharmacy.
5. **Meet Brandt (C4-03):** she explains Adrian's plan and the vaccine formula.
6. **Puzzles:** patient files, X-ray light box, blood sample analyser.
7. **Special room:** the director's office vault. Get the **vaccine prototype sample** and the **SMG**.
8. **Betrayal (C4-04):** Crane shoots Brandt, takes the sample, and leaves Mara with the Twin Surgeons.
9. **Boss: Twin Surgeons.**
10. **Escape cutscene (C4-06):** Mara breaks through the stairwell door to the rooftop helipad and takes the maintenance cable car across to Veyl Tower while the Revenant watches from the roof.

### Puzzles
| Puzzle | Hint source | Solution | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patient files | Three files with different admission dates | Combine room numbers in date order to get door code 2-1-7-0 | Records room |
| X-ray light box | Four X-rays, each with a numbered object hidden in the body | Order them by date to read 6-3-9-1 | Director's safe code |
| Blood analyser | Brandt's note: "Antibody glows violet when A and O mix" | Mix samples A and O | Vault lock released |

### Enemies
Runners, shamblers in scrubs, and the Revenant (patrols the whole middle section).

### Boss: Twin Surgeons
- Two fused surgeons, mutated into one body with two torsos and scalpel limbs.
- **Arena:** an operating theatre with surgical lights. The player can shoot out the lights to create darkness, which confuses them.
- **Phase 1:** both torsos attack. Destroy one torso's head.
- **Phase 2:** the surviving half tears free and is fast. Use the SMG and knock it down with leg shots.

### Files (6) and audio logs (2)
1. Brandt's resignation letter (unsent)
2. Patient file: Theo Kessler, "antibody positive, transfer to Tower"
3. Patient files A, B, C (code hint)
4. Director's memo: "Buyers arrive Friday. Demonstration must go ahead."
5. Nurse's diary
6. Crane's encrypted message (decoded in Chapter 5)
- Audio log: Brandt, "The formula needs living antibody. Theo's."
- Audio log: Theo's voicemail 3, "If you're hearing this, I'm in the Tower."

## Chapter 5: Veyl Tower

**Question answered:** How do we stop it?
**Setting:** Veyl Tower. Luxury upper floors, server floors, and the underground bio-lab beneath the building.

### Flow
1. **Arrival cutscene (C5-01):** the cable car crashes into a mid-level atrium. Adrian appears on all screens: "Welcome, Ms. Kessler. You brought me the second sample."
2. **Office floors:** **Veyl soldiers** (armed, using cover tactics) and mutated staff. Mixed stealth and combat, since soldiers fight both Mara and hosts.
3. **Executive lounge (safe room 5, the last Trader visit).**
4. **Puzzles:** the clearance card chain and server routing.
5. **Crane confrontation (C5-03):** Crane is cornered by the Revenant and infected. Mara takes the vaccine sample back from him.
6. **Mid-boss: Revenant final form** in the server hall. It can be killed now, using the generator overload.
7. **Special room:** the core lab. Free **Theo** (C5-05). Collect the **Magnum**. Final puzzle: synthesise the vaccine.
8. **FINAL BOSS: Adrian Veyl.**
9. **Ending:** a timed escape to the helipad as the lab self-destructs, then the ending cutscene and the post-credits scene.

### Puzzles
| Puzzle | Hint source | Solution | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clearance chain | Each card's floor is listed on the previous card's employee | Red card (floor 12) → blue card (floor 23) → gold card (floor 38) | Lift to the server floor |
| Server routing | Network map on the wall | Route the uplink through 3 server racks while avoiding the "bloomed" rack | Opens the core lab doors |
| Vaccine synthesis | Brandt's formula (Chapter 4) + vaccine sample + Theo's blood + the Station Zero catalyst (Chapter 3) | Three-step mixing sequence | Vaccine round for the final boss |

### Enemies
Veyl soldiers, runners, shamblers in suits, and the Revenant until its final fight.

### Mid-boss: Revenant (final form)
- The suit breaks open to show its regenerating core.
- **Arena:** the server hall with 4 generators.
- **Kill:** damage the core, then overload the generators while it stands on a grate.

### FINAL BOSS: Adrian Veyl
- **Phase 1 (human):** Adrian is armed, using a sniper rifle and turrets, and fighting from the lab's mezzanine. Disable the turrets and flush him out.
- **Phase 2 (partial Bloom):** he injects himself. He has regeneration and fast lunges. Shoot the exposed growths, as he regenerates otherwise.
- **Phase 3 (full Bloom):** a giant mass fused with the airborne dispersal tower. The arena changes as the floor breaks into platforms. Destroy the 3 dispersal valves, then fire the **vaccine round** into his core with the grenade launcher or Magnum.
- **Finisher cutscene (C5-07).**

### Files (6) and audio logs (2)
1. Buyer list (sequel hook)
2. Bloom Protocol summary
3. Crane's decoded message: "Mara is the backup sample."
4. Adrian's journal: "My father was too timid."
5. Clearance card memos (hint chain)
6. Theo's final note, written on the inside of his cell wall
- Audio log: Adrian's speech to buyers
- Audio log: Brandt's last log, "Tell Mara I'm sorry."
